# Advanced

Material for a reader who is past the guides and wants to see the design under load.

Every other page in this documentation shows a fragment — one resource, one scenario, one block of
`atf.yaml`. Nowhere is a suite shown whole. This section fixes that, and the suite it shows is ATF's
own.

[How ATF tests itself](how-atf-tests-itself.md) is that suite in full: layout, manifest, resources,
vocabulary and specs, with ATF as the system under test. Read it to see how the parts sit together
in something real, and for the two disciplines that keep a self-test honest.
